How it works

Getting feedback takes three steps and about a minute.

  1. 1

    Record or upload

    Prop your phone up so your whole body is in the frame, then film a few reps or upload a clip you already have.

  2. 2

    AI checks your form

    Pose tracking finds key points on your body, like your hips, knees, and shoulders, and measures the angles between them on every rep.

  3. 3

    Get feedback

    You get a short report showing what you did well, what to fix, and one tip to try on your next set.

Featured exercise

The squat: common mistakes

The squat is one of the most useful exercises you can learn, and one of the easiest to get wrong. The mistakes Move Smart flags most often are knees caving inward, heels lifting off the floor, and the lower back rounding at the bottom of the rep.

Each one moves the work away from your legs and puts extra stress on your knees or spine. Fixing them usually takes a lighter weight and a slower, more controlled rep.

Quick form tips

  • Keep your chest up and your back straight
  • Push your knees out in line with your toes
  • Lower with control instead of dropping
  • Breathe out as you push back up
