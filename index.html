Move Smart uses your webcam to count your reps and point out form mistakes as they happen, like lifted heels or a sagging back. Your video stays on your device.

How it works

Getting feedback takes three steps and about a minute.

  1. 1

    Set up your camera

    Stand 6 to 8 feet from your webcam so your whole body fits in the frame, or upload a clip you already recorded.

  2. 2

    Move Smart tracks your joints

    It finds 33 points on your body, like your hips, knees, and shoulders, and measures the angles between them on every rep.

  3. 3

    Fix it on the next rep

    When something slips, a tip appears right away. After your set, you get a summary of what to work on.

Try it without a camera

Drag the slider to squat. Then add a mistake and watch Move Smart catch it, using the same checks as the live camera.
