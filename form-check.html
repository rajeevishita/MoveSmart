    What Move Smart measures

    Joint angles
    The angle at your knees, hips, and elbows in every frame, to show how deep and how controlled each rep is.
    Alignment
    Whether your knees track over your toes and your back stays in a straight line.
    Range of motion
    How far you move on each rep, compared with the target depth for the exercise.
    Reps and tempo
    How many reps you do and how long each one takes, so you can spot when you start to rush.
