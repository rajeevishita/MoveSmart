Frequently asked questions

Do I need special equipment?

No. A phone with a camera and enough space to step back 6 to 8 feet is all you need.

Which exercises can Move Smart check?

The live form check works with squats and push-ups right now. The Exercises page has guides for the squat, push-up, lunge, deadlift, and overhead press.

Is my video private?

Yes. Move Smart only uses your joint positions, and you decide whether to save each clip.
