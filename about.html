About Move Smart

Move Smart helps people train safely by giving them the kind of form feedback that usually takes a personal trainer.

The problem

Most people who exercise can't see their own form. Small mistakes, like knees caving in or a rounded back, add up over time and can lead to pain or injury. Personal trainers can spot these problems, but they are expensive and aren't there for most workouts, especially at home.

How Move Smart started

Move Smart began as a student project: an exercise form analyzer that used an ordinary webcam and computer vision to track body movement. It was built with Python, OpenCV, and Google's MediaPipe pose tracking. Seeing how much useful feedback could come from a regular camera turned that project into the idea for Move Smart.

What we believe

  • Feedback should be simple

    One clear tip you can use right away helps more than a page of numbers.

  • Good form is for everyone

    You shouldn't need a gym membership or a personal trainer to learn how to move well.

  • Your videos are yours

    Move Smart only needs your joint positions, and you decide what gets saved.
