How it works

Move Smart turns a short video of your workout into clear feedback on your form. Here is what happens from the moment you press record.

Four steps to better form

  1. 1

    Set up your camera

    Place your phone 6 to 8 feet away at about hip height, so your whole body stays in the frame from head to feet. A side view works best for most exercises.

  2. 2

    Record your set

    Do 3 to 10 reps at your normal pace. You can record live or upload a clip you already have.

  3. 3

    AI tracks your movement

    Pose tracking finds key points on your body in every frame, like your shoulders, hips, knees, and ankles. Move Smart connects them into a simple skeleton and measures the angles between them.

  4. 4

    Read your report

    Your report shows how many reps you did, which ones looked good, and the one thing to fix first, with a short cue to try on your next set.

Setting up your camera

  • Stand 6 to 8 feet from your phone.
  • Keep the phone level and at about hip height.
  • Use a side view for squats, lunges, deadlifts, and push-ups. Use a front view to check whether your knees cave in.
  • Wear fitted clothes so your joints are easy to see.
  • Pick a bright room and avoid standing in front of a window.

Your privacy

Move Smart only needs the positions of your joints to check your form. Your videos stay private to your account, you choose whether to save each clip, and you can delete your clips at any time.
